**Leil** is an [[Elves|Elven]] city located on the northwestern coast of [[Alsunn]]. It serves as the entry-point for the [[Elven High Command]]. Leil has a strong military culture, with its inhabitants considering it the "last line of defense before the human blight arrives in [[S'elwein]]." Leil boasts the largest Elven barracks outside of S'elwein.

The city's economy revolves around soldiers returning from expeditions and paying for the services of locals. These services range from exotic cuisine to elaborate brothels. 

Many [[Humans|Human]] soldiers joke that capturing Leil (and its inhabitants) will make the war worth it.

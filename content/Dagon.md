**Dagon**, also known as The Favored Son or The Bullet Dragon, is a [[Dragonborn]] bounty hunter and [[Peace Marshal]] operating in [[The Southern Colonies]].

Dagon was born the son of a Dragonborn diplomat from a western tribe led by a silver dragon. He spent half of his childhood within The Southern Colonies and quickly picked up Common and Elven.

As a teenager, Dagon trained under multiple well-known gunmen. He got a reputation for being a sharpshooter.

As an adult, Dagon moved to the Southern Colonies and became a bounty hunter.

Ever since the previous Peace Marshal died under suspicious circumstances, Dagon has served as the Peace Marshal for the Southern Colonies.

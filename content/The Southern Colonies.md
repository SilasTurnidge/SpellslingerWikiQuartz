**The Southern Colonies** are a collective of [[Elves|Elven]] colonies in the south-west of [[Alsunn]].

# Law
The Southern Colonies follow [[Common Law|Elven Common Law]] with the following exception:
## Contracts
Contracts are legal documents signed between parties witnessed by a cleric or other member of the cloth. Contracts only have to be registered with the cleric who witnesses the signing.

If a contractee unknowingly commits a crime when following a contract, the contractor takes the blame for the wrongs committed.

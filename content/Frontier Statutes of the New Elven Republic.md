***Codified Regulations for Settlement, Security, and Conduct in the New Continent Frontier Districts***

---
# Preamble
Recognizing the unique hazards, strategic pressures, and limited administrative reach of the Republic within the New Continent, the Senate issues the following statutes to govern conduct, property, security, and justice within designated Frontier Districts. These statutes hold the force of law in all regions placed under Frontier Mandate pursuant to Article VII of the Articles of the Republic.

---
# Title I: Settlement and Land Tenure
## Section 1. Frontier Claims
1. Any [[Articles of the Republic#Article V Citizenship and Civic Status|Full or Partial Citizen]] may stake a Frontier Claim of no more than 50 hectares of arable land or 200 hectares of grazing land.
2. [[Articles of the Republic#Article V Citizenship and Civic Status|Residents Under Protection]] may stake claims only with sponsorship from a Citizen.
3. Claims must be registered with the district magistrate within 30 days, or they shall be considered null.
4. Land containing sunstone deposits is exempt from civilian claim and shall revert to the Republic.

## Section 2. Abandonment of Claims
1. A claim is considered abandoned if left uninhabited for 90 days, except in cases of verified emergency evacuation.
2. Abandoned claims may be re-staked by any legal settler after inspection by a district surveyor.

## Section 3. Homestead Consolidation
1. Settlers may combine adjacent claims for defensive purposes.
2. Consolidated homesteads must designate a Custodian, responsible for militia compliance and reporting.

---
# Title II: Security and Militia Obligations
## Section 4. Mandatory Militia Service
1. All adult settlers (excluding children, the infirm, and recognized clergy) must serve in the district militia.
2. Goliaths may serve under clan leadership; elves and others shall muster under district captains.
3. Failure to appear for militia musters is punishable by fines or temporary seizure of arms.

## Section 5. Armament Requirements
1. Each homestead must maintain:
	1. One functional ranged weapon per adult;
	2. A reserve of 40 arrows or equivalent ammunition;
	3. At least one melee weapon capable of fending off wildlife or draconic lesser kin.
2. The Republic may subsidize armaments during wartime shortages.

## Section 6. Threat Protocols
1. Upon confirmed hostile dragon sighting within ten miles, all settlers must retreat to the nearest fortified point.
2. Failure to evacuate may result in republic liability waivers and fines imposed on surviving relatives.

---
# Title III: Criminal Law and Enforcement
## Section 7. Authority of Sheriffs
1. Sheriffs are empowered to:
	1. Raise posses;
	2. Conduct searches without warrant in immediate emergencies;
	3. Detain suspects for up to 72 hours before magistrate review;
	4. Enforce martial restrictions issued by military commanders.

## Section 8. Crimes Specific to Frontier Districts
1. Tampering with Sunstone Shipments — Punishable by imprisonment up to 10 years.
2. Unauthorized Use of Magic Above Third Level — Automatic referral to the Authority of Magical Practices.
3. Smuggling Across Dragon Lines — Punishable by exile or hard labor.
4. Desertion from Frontier Militia — Punishable by branding and compulsory service.
5. Harboring Enemy Agents — Considered treason; penalty determined by military tribunal.

---
# Title IV: Indigenous Relations
## Section 9. Rights of Non‑Hostile Indigenous Peoples
1. Tribes designated non‑hostile by Senate decree may:
	1. Travel freely within their ancestral territories;
	2. Engage in trade with settlers and merchants;
	3. Appeal grievances to district magistrates.
2. Indigenous settlements are exempt from militia obligation unless voluntarily entered.

## Section 10. Prohibited Acts
1. Settlers may not:
	1. Seize indigenous property without due contract;
	2. Engage in retaliatory raids without sheriff authorization;
	3. Sell firearms or enchanted weapons without license.
2. Violations are punishable by fines, forfeiture of claim, or imprisonment.

---
# Title V: Trade, Transport, and Resource Regulation
## Section 11. Trade Licenses
1. Merchants operating between homesteads or between districts must hold a Frontier Trade License.
2. Smuggling enchanted items, sunstone shards, or military goods is a major offense under Section 8.

## Section 12. Caravan Requirements
1. Caravans must:
	1. Travel in groups of three wagons minimum;
	2. Carry a trained outrider or scout;
	3. Register departure and expected return with district officials.

## Section 13. Sunstone Protection
1. All sunstone refining, trade, or handling must be overseen by the Sunstone Administration Bureau.
2. Unauthorized extraction is considered theft from the Republic.

---
# Title VI: Justice and Procedure
## Section 14. Frontier Courts
1. District magistrates hear civil disputes, minor criminal offenses, and property cases.
2. Serious crimes (murder, treason, arcane violations) shall be referred to Provincial Tribunals or military courts.

## Section 15. Trials and Evidence
1. Oaths may be sworn upon any temple recognized by the Republic.
2. Magical evidence is admissible only if attested by a registered practitioner.
3. Witnesses from indigenous tribes may give testimony through translators certified by the district.

## Section 16. Punishments
1. District magistrates shall determine penalties through existing law and precedence.
2. Penalties shall include, depending on severity:
	1. Fines;
	2. Restitution;
	3. Public service;
	4. Temporary imprisonment;
	5. Conscription into military forces;
	6. Exile beyond protected territories.

---
# Title VII: Emergency Provisions
## Section 17. Activation of Martial Restrictions
1. District commanders may invoke emergency authority when:
	1. Hostile dragons sighted within five miles;
	2. Enemy combatants detected nearby;
	3. A settlement is destroyed or abandoned.
2. During emergencies, commanders may override magistrates.

## Section 18. Curfews and Travel Restrictions
1. Commanders may impose night curfews, suspend travel, or confine settlers to fortified points.
2. Violations constitute misdemeanor offenses unless the region is under active threat.

---
# Title VIII: Amendments to Frontier Statutes
Amendments may be issued by the Senate or by joint decree of a Consul and a Provincial Governor, subject to Senate review within one year.

---
*These statutes govern all who dwell within the lawless reaches of the New Continent, until such time that peace prevails or provincial authority is fully established.*

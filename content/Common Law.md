**Common Law** is the term for the broad series of laws that are constructed on the basis of the purpose of government being the betterment of its people.

Although all implementations of Common Law are different, below are some general concepts present in most iterations of the idea.
# Theft
Theft is defined as the taking of another person's personal property for the purpose of depriving them of this property.
# Battery
Battery is defined as nonconsensual physical contact, even when the contact is not violent but merely threatening or offensive.
# Murder
Murder is defined as an act in which someone ends someone else's life intentionally without due cause.

Most jurisdictions charge murders with the death penalty.
## Attempted Murder
Many jurisdictions do not differentiate between murder and attempted murder.
## Manslaughter
Manslaughter is murder committed without ill-intent. It is often less severely punished.
# Contracts
Contracts are legal documents signed between parties witnessed by a cleric or other member of the cloth. Contracts only have to be registered with the cleric who witnesses the signing.

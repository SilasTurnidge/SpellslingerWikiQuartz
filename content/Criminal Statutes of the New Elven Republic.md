***Codified Regulations for the Crimes of the Land and their Punishments***

---
# Preamble
In the interest of promoting a prosperous and peaceful existence for all of its citizens, the Senate issues the following statutes to determine disallowed activities and their punishments. These statutes hold force of law in all regions governed by the New Elven Republic.

---
# Title I: Crimes Against The Realm
## Section 1. Punishment For Crimes Against The Realm
1. All crimes against the realm are crimes of the greatest order and are punishable by execution or life labor.

## Section 2. Treason
1. Whoever, owing allegiance to the New Elven Republic, levies war against them or adheres to their enemies, giving them aid or comfort within the New Elven Republic or elsewhere is guilty of treason, a crime against the realm.

## Section 3. Terrorism
1. Whoever unlawfully uses violence or the threat of violence to coerce the people of the New Elven Republic or its government is guilty of terrorism, a crime against the realm.

## Section 4. Genocide
1. Whoever commits violence with the purpose of destroying an entire people or culture is guilty of genocide, a crime against the realm.

## Section 5. Grand Witchcraft
1. Whoever knowingly creates, researches, or otherwise pursues a spell, artifact, or magical entity that poses a clear and present threat to the security of the New Elven Republic is guilty of grand witchcraft, a crime against the realm.

---
# Title II: Felony Crimes
## Section 6. Punishment For Felony Crimes
1. Felony crimes are divided into letter grades, with each corresponding to a different punishment range:
	1. Class A: The maximum penalty is life labor.
	2. Class B: The maximum penalty is 100 years labor.
	3. Class C: The maximum penalty is 50 years labor.
	4. Class D: The maximum penalty is 25 years labor.
	5. Class E: The maximum penalty is 10 years labor.
	6. Class F: The maximum penalty is 5 years labor.
	7. Class G: The maximum penalty is 1 year of labor.

## Section 7. Property Felony Crimes
1. Whoever illegally seizes the property of a Citizen worth more than 10 Platinum Pieces with the intent to deprive them thereof is guilty of grand larceny, a Class A felony.
2. Whoever unlawfully enters a building with the intent to commit a felony or theft is guilty of burglary, a Class D felony.
3. Whoever willingly and maliciously attempts to burn another's property is guilty of arson, a Class C felony.

## Section 8. Violent Felony Crimes
1. Whoever willingly, deliberately, and premeditatedly kills another person is guilty of first degree murder, a Class A felony.
2. Whoever willingly and maliciously kills another person (without premeditated intent) is guilty of second degree murder, a Class B felony.
3. Whoever unlawfully seizes the property of another with violent force is guilty of robbery, a Class E felony.
4. Whoever unlawfully has sexual intercourse with another without their consent and by means of fear, force, and coercion is guilty of rape, a class B felony.
5. Whoever unlawfully assaults or strikes another with a deadly weapon is guilty of aggravated assault, a class C felony.
6. Whoever knowingly creates, researches, or otherwise pursues a spell, artifact, or magical entity with intent to unlawfully harm another is guilty of witchcraft, a class C felony.

## Section 9. Other Felony Crimes
1. Whoever willingly fails to fulfill the terms of a contract without proper recourse is guilty of breach of contract, a Class G felony.
2. Whoever willingly or negligently misrepresents fact during the negotiatation of a contract is guilty of fraud, a class F felony.
3. Whoever willingly and knowingly lies while under oath in front of a magistrate is guilty of perjury, a Class D felony.
4. Whoever refuses to pay taxes is guilty of tax evasion, a Class F felony.
5. Whoever knowingly and purposely avoids being levied towards a war effort is guilty of conscription evasion, a Class B felony.

---
# Title III: Misdemeanor Crimes
## Section 10. Punishment For Misdemeanor Crimes
1. Misdemeanor crimes are divided into letter grades, with each corresponding to a different punishment range:
	1. Class A: The maximum penalty is 1 year labor.
	2. Class B: The maximum penalty is 6 months labor.
	3. Class C: The maximum penalty is 30 days labor.

## Section 11. Non-Violent Misdemeanor Crimes
1. Whoever illegally seizes the property of a Citizen worth less than 10 Platinum Pieces with the intent to deprive them thereof is guilty of larceny, a Class A misdemeanor.
2. Whoever conducts themselves in a way that disturbs the peace, morals, or safety of the public is guilty of disorderly conduct, a Class C misdemeanor.
3. Whoever physically invades a Citizen's real property or causes an object or third party to invade it is guilty of trespassing, a Class C misdemeanor.
4. Whoever purposefully defaces a piece of public or private property is guilty of vandalism, a Class C misdemeanor.
5. Whoever practices magic above the Third Level without registering with the Authority of Magical Practices is guilty of unregistered sorcery, a Class B misdemeanor.

## Section 12. Violent Misdemeanor Crimes
1. Whoever unknowingly and accidentally unlawfully kills another due to negligence is guilty of manslaughter, a Class A misdemeanor.
2. Whoever intentionally causes minor physical damage to another or creates a reasonable fear of harm is guilty of simple assault, a Class B misdemeanor.

---
# Title IV: Preventing Crimes
## Section 13. Self-Defense
1. A Citizen may invoke self-defense when they:
	1. Reasonably believe that they are about to suffer physical or property damage.
2. Self-defense grants the invoker legal permission to use force to defend themselves and their property without fear of legal retribution.
	1. Use of excessive force is not permitted

---
# Title V: Amendments to Criminal Statutes
Amendments may be issued by the Senate or by joint decree of a Consul and a Provincial Governor, subject to Senate review within one year.

---
*These statutes govern all who reside within the New Elven Republic.*

**S'elwein** is a city located on the northwest coast of [[Alsunn]]. It is the headquarters of the [[Elven High Command]]. S'elwein is the epitome of all that is Elven. Music, food, magic, government — all exists in S'elwein.

Many elves make the pilgrimage back to S'elwein to step on the golden footprint said to be the first elven step taken on the new continent.

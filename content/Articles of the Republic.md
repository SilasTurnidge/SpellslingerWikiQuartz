***Foundational Charter of the New Elven Republic***

---
# Preamble
In the aftermath of the Fall of the [[First Dynasty]] and in recognition of the shared sacrifices of [[Elves|elves]], [[Goliaths|goliaths]], and all free peoples who defended their homelands, the undersigned provinces and clans establish the [[New Elven Republic]]. We do so to preserve our independence, secure just governance, cultivate the arcane and material prosperity of our lands, and maintain vigilance against future aggressors. These Articles form the supreme law of the Republic.

---
# Article I: The Nature of the Republic
1. The New Elven Republic shall be a perpetual federation of provinces, clan-domains, and chartered municipalities.
2. Sovereignty belongs jointly to the [[Elven Senate|Senate]], the [[Elven Consulate|Consulate]], and the Provinces, each within their proper spheres.
3. The Republic recognizes the dignities of all lawful peoples within its borders, while reserving certain civic privileges to those deemed [[#Article V Citizenship and Civic Status|Qualified Citizens under Article V]].
4. All lands of the Republic are held under the protection of the Senate, and no foreign prince, temple, or warlord shall exercise authority within these borders.

---
# Article II: The Senate
1. Legislative power shall reside in the Elven Senate, composed of members known as Senators.
2. The Senate shall consist of forty seats, apportioned among the provinces according to the Census of Citizenship.
3. Senators shall serve terms of twelve years, with staggered rotation every three years.
4. The Senate shall:
	1. Enact statutory law for the Republic.
	2. Levy taxes, tariffs, and duties.
	3. Ratify treaties and oversee foreign affairs.
	4. Declare war and authorize military operations.
	5. Appoint Consuls, High Tribunal Justices, and principal officers of the Republic.
	6. Approve [[Peace Marshal|Peace Marshals]] appointed by the Consulate.
5. Proceedings of the Senate shall be recorded in the Great Ledger, preserved in the capital archives.

---
# Article III: The Consulate
1. Executive power shall be entrusted to two Consuls, elected by the Senate for terms of six years.
2. The Consuls shall be responsible for:
	1. Executing the laws of the Republic.
	2. Commanding the armed forces in concert with the Senate.
	3. Supervising the Sunstone Administration Bureau, Frontier Affairs Office, and all other civil agencies.
3. In times of grave peril, the Senate may grant one Consul an Emergency Mandate, permitting extraordinary authority for a period not exceeding one year.
4. No individual may serve more than two mandates as Consul.

---
# Article IV: The Judiciary
1. Judicial power shall be vested in:
	1. The High Tribunal of the Republic;
	2. The Provincial Tribunals;
	3. The Lower Courts as established by statute.
2. The High Tribunal shall consist of seven Justices appointed by the Senate for life tenures, removable only by supermajority of the Senate.
3. The High Tribunal shall have authority to:
	1. Interpret these Articles;
	2. Resolve disputes between provinces;
	3. Review capital judgments; and
	4. Determine the legality of Senate statutes and Consular decrees.
4. Provincial Tribunals shall oversee appellate review within their regions, [[#Article VII War Powers and Frontier Mandates|except in cases of military jurisdiction under Article VII]].

---
# Article V: Citizenship and Civic Status
1. The Republic recognizes three categories of civic status:
	1. Full Citizens;
	2. Partial Citizens;
	3. Residents Under Protection.
2. Full Citizenship may be held by:
	1. Elves by birth;
	2. Goliaths certified by the Examination of Competence;
	3. Others granted letters of elevation by a two-thirds Senate vote.
3. Partial Citizenship may be held by:
	1. Goliaths of recognized clans;
	2. Half-elves born within the Republic;
	3. Dwarves holding guild-standing.
4. Residents Under Protection include humans, foreign settlers, and non-hostile indigenous peoples. They enjoy legal protections and obligations but lack electoral and office-holding rights.
5. The Senate shall conduct a Census of Citizenship every twenty years.

---
# Article VI: The Provinces and Local Authority
1. Provinces shall retain control over:
	1. Local courts
	2. Militia organization
	3. Customs and property law
	4. Clan obligations (in goliath-majority regions)
2. No province may:
	1. Make alliances with foreign states;
	2. Coin money independently;
	3. Obstruct lawful movement of goods or persons across provincial boundaries.
3. Municipalities and clans may maintain customary law insofar as it does not contradict these Articles or statutory law.

---
# Article VII: War Powers and Frontier Mandates
1. The Senate may designate zones of the New Continent as Frontier Districts under military jurisdiction.
2. Military commanders within Frontier Districts may:
	1. Enforce martial law during active threat;
	2. Requisition supplies necessary for survival and defense;
	3. Establish temporary courts-martial for crimes threatening the security of the district.
3. All extraordinary measures must be reported to the Senate within thirty days.
4. [[#Article III The Consulate|The Emergency Mandate of Article III]] may suspend Articles [[#Article VI The Provinces and Local Authority|VI]] and [[#Article VIII Individual Rights and Protections|VIII]] within declared war zones.

---
# Article VIII: Individual Rights and Protections
The following rights shall be guaranteed to all persons within the Republic, except during lawfully declared emergencies:
1. The right to petition the Senate and local authorities.
2. The right to a hearing before a lawful magistrate.
3. Protection from arbitrary arrest and confiscation.
4. Sanctuary in temples recognized by the Republic, subject to Senate regulation.
5. Freedom of scholarly, arcane, and religious inquiry, except where such practice threatens the security of the Republic.
6. The right to travel between provinces, except in wartime-restricted zones.

---
# Article IX: Administration of Magic and Arcana
1. The practice of magic shall be regulated by the Authority of Magical Practices (herein referred to as the AMP).
2. Mages, druids, clerics, and other spellcasters must register spellcasting above the Third Level.
3. Sunstone research and extraction shall remain a state-regulated activity.
4. Magical coercion, binding, or enchantment of citizens is prohibited except under sanctioned wartime operations.
5. Violations of arcane law fall under the joint jurisdiction of the Provincial Tribunals and the AMP.

---
# Article X: Amendments and Revisions
1. Amendments to these Articles require:
	1. A two-thirds vote of the Senate, and
	2. Ratification by two-thirds of the provincial legislatures.
2. No amendment may abolish the Senate, the High Tribunal, or the basic structure of civic status without unanimous provincial consent.

---
# Article XI: Ratification
These Articles shall take effect upon approval by the provisional Senate and ratification by the founding provinces and clans. All officials of the Republic shall swear an Oath of Fidelity to uphold this Charter.

---
*Thus is founded the New Elven Republic, to endure until the light of peace returns to Ena and the New Continent.*

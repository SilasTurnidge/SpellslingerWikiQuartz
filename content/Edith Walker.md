**Edith Walker** is the [[Peace Marshal]] for [[The Western Territory]]. Born a [[Half-Elf]] to a [[Humans|Human]] noble and an [[Elves|Elven]] [[Senator]], Edith grew up in human territory before disavowing her human lineage at the beginning of the [[Third Human-Elven War]].

Due to the Elven distrust, she serves as a Peace Marshal instead of a soldier.

Even in an era of firearms, Edith still wields a special crossbow.

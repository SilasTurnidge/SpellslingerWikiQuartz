**Dael** is an [[Elves|Elven]] city located on the southwest coast of [[Alsunn]] in [[The Southern Colonies]]. It is the most well-known city within the colonies.

Although Dael is considered Elven territory, it is governed by the Elder Dragon [[Eureka]].

Dael's economy is said to have two pillars: travel and trade. Locals claim that a third pillar exists: crime.

Many peoples indigenous to Alsunn make a living by giving tours of the continent to outsiders. This is especially common in Dael.

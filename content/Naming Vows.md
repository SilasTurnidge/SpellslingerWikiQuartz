**Naming Vows** are a series of magically-enforced vows that [[Dragons]] take when they come of age.

The specifics of the vows depend on the name the dragon wishes to obtain, as words hold a special significance in draconic society. However, all naming vows generally include the following:
1. A pledge to uphold the responsibility of the name until death.
2. A pledge to better the world for dragons at-large.
3. A pledge to uphold the rulings of [[The Supreme Elders]].

**Dendae** is an [[Elves|Elven]] town on the west coast of [[Alsunn]] in [[The Curling Mountains]]. The city is commonly divided into East Dendae and West Dendae due to the wealth disparity.

The city was historically important due to a [[Sunstone]] vein located there that has since been depleted.

# East Dendae
East Dendae is centered around the mining of Sunstone. However, due to years of mining the once easily-accessible veins have since retreated deep underground.

For a long period of time, denizens of East Dendae had to risk their lives deep within the mines for a chance at surviving the year.

The war effort has since re-invigorated the economy of East Dendae, as the [[Elven High Command]] has repurposed the unused mines as barracks for soldiers.
# West Dendae
West Dendae is a first and foremost a vacation spot for wealthy elves. The large deposit of Sunstone in the area causes the sand to glow golden and emanate a sun-like warmth. People suffering from [[Mana Deficiency Disorder]] can benefit greatly from sitting on the sands of West Dendae.

Demand has driven the real-estate price up so far that locals to Dendae had to move to the east side. At one point real-estate bubble got so high that it was believed one apartment in West Dendae cost more than a castle in most of [[Ena]].

Recently, the Elven High Command has begun requisitioning unoccupied houses for military officers to stay in. This is deeply unpopular with the upper echelon of elven society, though they would never express it.

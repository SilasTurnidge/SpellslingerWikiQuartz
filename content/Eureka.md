**Elder-General Eureka** (born Almno) is an Elder [[Dragons|Dragon]] Archmage living in and ruling the city of [[Dael]].

Even among dragons, Eureka is considered especially adept at the arcane. In fact, many dragons refuse to meet Eureka in person for fear of having their mind read.

Eureka gained renown amongst [[Hominid|hominids]] after it overrode it's own [[Naming Vows]] to rename itself after a popular saying among [[Sunstone]] prospectors. When an [[Elves|Elven]] reporter asked the dragon why it broke the longstanding tradition, the draconic archmage told the reporter that he did it because "it sounded funny." Although this led to [[The Supreme Elders]] disavowing and excommunicating Eureka, other dragons continued to meet with him in secret.

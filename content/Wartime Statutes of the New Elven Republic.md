***Extraordinary Laws Enacted Under the Wartime Mandate of the Senate***

---
# Preamble
In recognition of the Third Human–Elven War, the dangers posed by Triumvirate forces, the hazards of the Long Plains, and the existential threat of draconic dominions along the New Continent coasts, the Senate of the New Elven Republic decrees the following Wartime Statutes. These statutes possess immediate legal force in all Provinces, Frontier Districts, and military jurisdictions, superseding civil statutes when conflict arises.

---
# Title I: General Provisions of Wartime Governance
## Section 1. Activation of Wartime Authority
1. Wartime statutes shall be active until formally suspended by a majority vote of the Senate.
2. The Senate may delegate emergency wartime powers to one Consul, granting Emergency Mandate authority as set forth in the Articles of the Republic.
3. Provincial [[Peace Marshal|Peace Marshals]] and governors shall enact wartime directives upon receipt without delay.

## Section 2. Hierarchy of Wartime Law
1. Military directives override provincial law.
2. Senate edicts override Consular orders unless issued under Emergency Mandate.
3. Civil courts shall defer to military tribunals in all matters pertaining to security, espionage, or treason.

---
# Title II: Conscription, Levies, and National Service
## Section 3. General Conscription
1. The Republic may conscript:
	1. All Full Citizens between ages 20 and 120;
	2. Partial Citizens between ages 18 and 100;
	3. Residents Under Protection only in Frontier Districts.
2. Religious officials and essential artificers may receive exemptions.

## Section 4. Special Levies
1. Each Province shall raise additional levies equal to 2% of its Census of Citizenship for the war effort.
2. Goliath clans may fulfill levy quotas through designated champions.

## Section 5. Duration of Service
1. A standard service term is defined as five years.
2. Conscripts in active war zones may be retained for up to three additional years.

---
# Title III: Powers of Military Command
## Section 6. Command Authority
1. Military commanders may:
	1. Establish martial law in active-threat regions;
	2. Requisition supplies, livestock, magical foci, and transport animals;
	3. Press individuals into temporary labor for fortification or emergency duties.

## Section 7. Military Jurisdiction
1. Military courts have authority over:
	1. Treason and sedition;
	2. Collaboration with enemy agents;
	3. Desertion and insubordination;
	4. Unauthorized magical warfare;
	5. Interference with military operations.
2. Sentences may include execution, exile, hard labor, or service in penal battalions.

---
# Title IV: Arcane Warfare Regulations
## Section 8. Registration of Battle Mages
1. All casters capable of Fourth Level magic or higher must register as Arcane Combatants.
2. Failure to register constitutes evasion of wartime duty.

## Section 9. Prohibited Magical Practices
1. The following are forbidden without Consular authorization:
	1. Summoning beyond the Fifth Circle;
	2. Spells that alter weather patterns in populated zones;
	3. Enchantments placed on officers of the Republic;
	4. Necromantic use of enemy dead.

## Section 10. Sunstone Allocation for War Effort
1. All sunstone production not required for civil survival is diverted to:
	1. Transportation networks;
	2. Arcane shielding projects;
	3. Anti-draconic artillery development.
2. Unauthorized possession of refined sunstone is punishable by military tribunal.

---
# Title V: Internal Security and Counter-Espionage
## Section 11. Identification and Pass Requirements
1. Anyone traveling between provinces must carry a Wartime Pass.
2. Citizens of enemy states must undergo additional screening.

## Section 12. Sedition and Collaboration
1. Spreading defeatist rumor, interfering with recruitment, or advocating surrender constitutes sedition.
2. Collaborating with enemy forces is punishable by execution or life labor.

## Section 13. Counter-Espionage Authority
1. The Office of Internal Vigilance may conduct searches, intercept messages, and detain individuals suspected of aiding the enemy.
2. Warrants are not required in active-threat zones.

---
# Title VI: Civilians in War Zones
## Section 14. Evacuation and Relocation
1. Civilian populations in threatened regions may be evacuated by force.
2. Refugees shall be relocated to secure districts and assigned temporary citizenship status.

## Section 15. Protected Civilian Status
1. Civilians within designated Safe Corridors are exempt from militia duty.
2. Attacks on Safe Corridor infrastructure constitute war crimes.

## Section 16. Looting and Property Seizure
1. Looting of civilian property is prohibited.
2. Military requisitions must be documented and compensated when feasible.

---
# Title VII: Logistics, Supply, and Resource Management
## Section 17. Strategic Material Controls
1. Iron, timber, medicinal herbs, and alchemical reagents critical to the war must be registered with district officials.
2. Hoarding is punishable by fines and seizure of goods.

## Section 18. Transportation Priority
1. Military caravans, arcane supply wagons, and sunstone convoys have right-of-way on all roads.
2. Obstructing a military convoy constitutes a Class A felony.

---
# Title VIII — Discipline, Morale, and Conduct
## Section 19. Conduct of Soldiers
1. Soldiers must maintain discipline, obey command, and refrain from harming civilian populations.
2. Fraternization with enemy combatants is forbidden.

## Section 20. Incentives for Valor
1. Soldiers demonstrating extraordinary valor may receive:
	1. Early discharge;
	2. Land grants on the New Continent;
	3. Recognition in the Ledger of Service.

--- 
# Title IX — Wartime Amendments
1. The Senate may issue emergency amendments without provincial ratification.
2. All wartime amendments must be reviewed within three years of conflict’s end.

---
*These statutes shall remain in effect until the Senate declares the end of wartime conditions or until superseded by future enactments.*

**Alon Widae** is an [[Archmage]] and the [[Peace Marshal]] for the greater [[Elven High Command]] area. Alon is exceptionally old even for an [[Elves|Elf]], having been born near the beginning of [[The Elven Millenium]], nearly 3000 years ago.

Alon founded [[Widae Academy]], the largest magic-learning institution in [[Alsunn]].

**Qarsath** is an [[Elves|Elven]] city located on the west coast of [[Alsunn]] in [[The Western Territory]]. It is a port city that specializes in shipping and trade. Before the war, a rumor circulated among some [[Humans]] that Qarsath translated to "merchant heaven." This is false, Qarsath just means "city of paths."
# Old Qarsath
When elven settlers first arrived in Qarsath the marsh terrain made it impractical to build traditional houses on land. The settlers instead recreated a form of [[Orcs|Orcish]] building called a [[Mudhouse]]. These lightweight buildings would rise and fall with the tides and earthquakes.
# New Qarsath
After trade proliferated in Qarsath, many rich merchants flocked to the city. These merchants opted to build slightly offshore to allow for larger, more imposing structures.
## The Imperial Academy
One of the first buildings constructed in the water was the imperial academy, a secluded military school off the coast of Qarsath. The academy's most impressive feat is their ability to train a cadet in foreign languages incredibly quickly. Cadets are able to attain fluency in most languages within 3 months of intense study.
